About Us

Our Objective

Our main objective is to unify all the committees at TSEC and provide a single platform where students can discover events, workshops, and opportunities across the campus. We aim to enhance collaboration, engagement, and participation among students to make college life more productive and enjoyable.

Who We Are

We are students of Thadomal Shahani Engineering College studying Information Technology. We created a website that brings all the college committees together in one place. The goal is to make life simpler for every TSEC student and keep everyone in the loop. On this platform, you will never miss out on announcements, workshops, cultural festivals, or fun events. It’s a hub where students can explore, engage, and stay connected with everything happening on campus. By connecting committees and students, we hope to create a more vibrant and collaborative community. The website is easy to use and designed for quick access to updates. Overall, it makes being part of TSEC more fun and engaging.

This platform also gives students a real chance to get involved. You can check out committee members, see what projects are ongoing, and find upcoming events. It makes it simple for students to participate, share ideas, and show off their skills. The website encourages teamwork, creativity, and discovering opportunities you might not have noticed before. It’s a space where everyone can get inspired and motivated to be part of college life. By getting involved, students can make meaningful connections and build experiences beyond just academics.

The website also makes campus life easier for new and returning students. New students can quickly get a feel for the campus and committees. Returning students can stay updated on all the activities and events happening throughout the year. Everything is organized in one place so no one misses out. It helps students plan their participation and stay connected effortlessly. The platform also improves communication between committees and students in a fun and interactive way. Our goal is to build a TSEC community that is lively, inclusive, and full of opportunities for everyone.

FAQs

You can join a committee by reaching out to the committee members listed on our platform or registering through upcoming events.

Most workshops are free, but some special events may have limited seats or a small fee. Check the event details for accurate information.

All announcements are updated on our platform. You can also subscribe to notifications to get instant updates.
